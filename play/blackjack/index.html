House rules
  • Bets are 1 to 100 GOLD. Each hand is dealt from its own shuffled 52-card deck.
  • The dealer draws to 17 and stands on every 17, soft 17 included.
  • A natural (an ace and a ten-card as your first two) pays 3:2, rounded down to the nearest 0.01 GOLD. Natural against natural is a push. Any other win pays 1:1; a tie is a push.
  • You may double on your first two cards: you put up the bet again and get exactly one more card.
  • No splitting, insurance or surrender.
  • The dealer's second card is dealt only after you finish, so there is no peek: a dealer natural beats a doubled hand and takes both stakes.
  • From 25 Oct 2026 08:00 UTC: if the house has not dealt your card within 5 minutes, the hand is paid its best result: 2.5x the bet, or 2x on a doubled hand. If you leave your turn for 10 minutes, the house stands for you.
